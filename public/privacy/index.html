Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Spec Clash (spec-clash.vercel.app) is a free car specification comparison site run by an individual in Perth, Western Australia. This page explains what information the site handles and why.

Browsing the site

  • You don't need an account to compare cars. We don't ask for your name, email or any contact details.
  • We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you personally.
  • When you click through to a car listing, we log the make and model clicked (not who clicked) so we can see which links are useful.
  • Your recent comparisons may be saved in your own browser's local storage so the site remembers them. That data stays on your device.

TikTok integration

The site owner uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to send Spec Clash comparison videos to the @specclash05 TikTok account as drafts. If you connect a TikTok account:

  • We request only the user.info.basic and video.upload permissions.
  • The access token TikTok issues is stored in an encrypted, http-only cookie in your browser. It isn't stored in a database or shared with anyone.
  • It's used only to upload videos you choose to your TikTok drafts. Nothing is posted publicly by us; you finish and publish each post in the TikTok app.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the queue page, by clearing your cookies, or by removing Spec Clash from TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent any information.
  • We don't use advertising trackers.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel processes standard request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site, under its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a direct message to @specclash05 on TikTok.
